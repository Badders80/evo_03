## The Cast

### `char_emma` — The Syndicate Owner
- 30yo modern professional, quiet luxury aesthetic. Dark hair in neat low bun, tailored camel wool coat, cream cashmere turtleneck, minimal gold jewelry.
- **Role:** Relatable aspirational audience surrogate. The viewer's eyes.

### `char_jack` — The Master Trainer
- 55yo seasoned horseman. Weathered features, short silver hair, olive Barbour wax jacket, tweed flat cap, leather-strapped stopwatch.
- **Role:** Authority, insider expertise, trust. Speaks in short quoted lines ("She's primed today. Plan is simple: sit off the pace, strike at the turn.")

### `char_tyler` — The Track Jockey
- 24yo athletic jockey. Focused gaze, clean-shaven, navy & gold silks, helmet with unclipped strap.
- **Role:** Action, speed, race-day energy. Quoted lines are teammate-warm ("See you in the Winner's Circle, lads.")

### `char_nellie` — The Star Racehorse
- 3yo dark bay thoroughbred filly. Distinctive white star blaze on forehead, lean muscular frame, glossy coat.
- **Role:** Hero subject. Lead with her — the thoroughbred is never outranked by any human or interface in frame.

## Character rules (locked)

1. Every character is fictional. Fictional-flag on every script that quotes one.
2. No character carries the founder's name (a script colleague named "Alex" is
   renamed, e.g. "Sam").
3. Characters never mention price, dollar figures, or purchase mechanics.
4. Characters express the triad through behaviour (game plans, photos, ritual),
   never through sales talk.
