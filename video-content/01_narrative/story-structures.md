## Structure A — The Audio Snap (violent audio contrast)

Pattern interrupt: loud chaos → dead silence → intimate reality. Hooks at
velocity, snaps attention to zero, lands the human truth.

```
[0–3s:  BRASH & LOUD]   Quoted FICTIONAL race announcer at full hype +
                        thundering hooves + high-velocity B-roll (+2dB)
[3–3.2s: HARD CUT]      Audio drops to dead silence for 0.2s
[3–7s:  SOFT & REAL]    Quiet yard ambient, coffee pouring, intimate VO
                        (SFX -12dB / VO 0dB)
[7–10s: THE CALL]       Calm Private Banker closing line + brand outro
```

**Rule:** the brash announcer is a quoted fictional character (a commentator
at the race) — never brand voice. Brand VO is always Private Banker Standard.
**The price is never the closing line. The feeling is.**

## Structure B — The 4-Beat Commercial (30s)

Maps to 4MAT (WHY → WHAT → HOW → WHAT IF):

1. **Beat 1 (0:00–0:06) — The Awakening:** gallop through dawn mist
   (`cambridge_dawn` + `nellie` + `tyler`).
2. **Beat 2 (0:06–0:14) — Insider Access:** morning barn inspection
   (`stables` + `jack` + `emma`).
3. **Beat 3 (0:14–0:22) — The Owner's Ritual:** checking live status over

4. **Beat 4 (0:22–0:30) — The Victory Payoff:** winner's circle toast and
   sash draping (`parade_ring` + `prop_sash` + `prop_trophy_table`).
   Close on **"Own the Experience."**

## The 5-Element Prompt Formula (verbatim from source)

```
[1. Camera Movement] + [2. Core Subject & Action] + [3. Setting & Environment]
+ [4. Tech/Interface Overlay (quiet rail only)] + [5. Cinematic Style Anchor]
```

## The 3 Golden Rules (verbatim — the anti-drift core)

1. **Keep text overlays out of the generation prompt.** Models mangle text;
   generate clean B-roll, add text in post.
2. **Reuse the identical style anchor** across every prompt so clips cut
   together seamlessly: `35mm lens, high shutter speed sports photography,
   broadcast 4K, photorealistic`.
3. **Keep prompts under 60 words.** Concise physical descriptors only.
