**Job:** ritual — ownership woven into the ordinary morning.

## VO + close

- **VO slot (≤12 words):** locked line — *"Real trainer updates. Real morning
  rituals. Become an Owner."*
- **Close:** **"Own the Experience."**
- **Form law:** the phone ever shows live status + horse name only — never
  odds, never a market, never a form guide.
- **Fictional flag:** required — trainer voice memo is a quoted fictional
  character.
