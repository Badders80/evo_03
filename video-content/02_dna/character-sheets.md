# Character Sheets — Locked Visual Identity Blocks

**Status:** DRAFT v1 (2026-08-28) — derives from LOCKED `01_narrative/characters.md`
+ `02_dna/crowd-and-venue.md` (LOCKED v1, R1–R5). Founder sign-off → LOCKED v1.
**Purpose:** one paste-ready physical identity block per character. These
blocks are the "Core Subject" element of the 5-element prompt formula —
paste identical into every prompt featuring that character so faces, kit and
silhouette never drift between clips.
**Binding rules carried from `characters.md`:** every character is FICTIONAL
(`noBorrowedAuthority`); fictional-flag on every script quoting one; no
character mentions price, dollars, or purchase mechanics.

## `char_nellie` — The Star Racehorse (hero subject)

**Default block:**

```
Dark bay thoroughbred filly, three years old, glossy summer coat, distinctive

```

**Living-shape register (form law, crowd-and-venue §8):** her coat, stride,

**Consistency anchors:** white star blaze (unique identifier, every scene) ·
dark bay coat · braids on race days only (unbraided at dawn yard — texture
truth).
**Job:** hero. She is never outranked by any human or interface in frame;
the camera is inside the rail with her.

## Cross-character constants

- **Fictional-flag** on every script quoting any of the four — no real-stable,
  real-trainer, or real-jockey endorsement implication.
- **No price rule:** no character ever mentions price, dollars, or purchase
  mechanics (Fight Club rule).
- **Register discipline:** yard scenes use yard blocks; race-day scenes use
  race-day blocks; never blend registers in one scene.
- **Silhouette test:** each character must be identifiable from silhouette +
  one anchor alone (bun/blazer · silver hair/stopwatch · silks · star blaze)
  — this is what survives motion blur and fast cutting.
- **Negative-prompt block** from `crowd-and-venue.md` §11 appends to every
  character prompt (no brand logos, no readable text, no crowd faces in
  focus).
